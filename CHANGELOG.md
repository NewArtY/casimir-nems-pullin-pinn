## 1.3.0 - 2026-10-22 (revised Physical Review E manuscript)

Published 2026-10-22 as `10.5281/zenodo.23575743`; the manuscript cites this
version DOI.

Answers a pre-submission review. No earlier result file is changed except the
figures and tables listed below.

### Added

- `src/threshold_checks.py`: the from-rest threshold by three independent
  methods (fixed-step RK4 at five step/horizon pairs; adaptive DOP853 with a
  horizon-free classification by two finite-time certificates, saddle
  crossing and energy below the saddle; backward continuation of the stable
  manifold of the saddle, with no forward orbit), the band width at
  `beta = 0` toward `zeta_c` by the last two, the slope of the threshold by
  Richardson-extrapolated finite differences, `zeta_c(beta)` for release from
  0 and from the zero-bias rest position, the damping `zeta_0(beta)` below
  which the zero-bias orbit collapses for `beta > beta_d`, an explicit
  counterexample to the limit of Flores (2017), and the delayed pull-in of
  Fig. 1(b). Writes `results/threshold_checks.json` and
  `results/zeta_c_scan*.json`.
- `src/pinn_checks.py`: residuals of the pull-in network on the collocation
  points and on a 125-fold denser grid, the local Gronwall horizons with the
  dense-grid residual, and an ablation in the raw parametrization at the same
  budget (`models/pinn_pullin_raw.pth`). Writes `results/pinn_checks.json`.
- `src/matsubara_bound.py`: numerical check of the zero-frequency bound on
  the thermal Casimir pressure of ideal mirrors (Appendix A).
- `src/appendix_numbers.py`: the numbers of Sec. III and Appendix B that no
  other stage produced (equilibrium count, collapse-exponent fits, `q_f`,
  bottleneck ratios, saddle-escape slope, phase-diagram row fits, constants
  of the a posteriori bound). Writes `results/appendix_numbers.json`.
- `src/conductivity_estimate.py`: plasma-model Lifshitz reduction factor for
  gold at each device gap and the step thresholds with `beta` rescaled by it.
  Writes `results/conductivity_estimate.json`.
- `src/surrogate_validation.py`: refits the damped-threshold surrogate of
  Fig. 3 (reproducing the archived slopes exactly), saves its weights to
  `models/dyn_boundary_surrogate.pth`, and compares its slope with the
  separatrix slope on a grid of step 0.0025 in `beta`. Writes
  `results/surrogate_validation.json`.
- `run_all.py` stages `devices` (Table S7 was not regenerated by any stage)
  and `sm_tables`; `make_sm_tables.py` gained a `main()`.
- Tables S11 (threshold by three methods) and S12 (`zeta_c(beta)`).

### Changed

- Fig. 1(b): the point (`alpha = 0.0724`, `beta = 0.0407`, `zeta = 1`) lies
  above the fold (`alpha_c = 0.0682`) and is a delayed pull-in, not an
  approach to equilibrium; the reference is now drawn through to contact at
  `tau = 38.84`. The network and its archive name `growing` are unchanged.
- Table S8 now comes from the separatrix construction, with the certificate
  bisection as an uncertainty column, and extends to `zeta = 0.39`.
  `src/band_scan.py` and `results/band_scan.csv` are kept but no longer
  feed a table.
- Table S1 header and Table S7 caption.
- `run_all.py` stages `thr_checks`, `pinn_checks`, `matsubara`.

### Added

- `src/damping_regimes.py`: squeeze-film damping of the representative
  devices in air (isothermal Reynolds film on a square plate, Blech 1983,
  with the rarefied-gas viscosity of Veijola et al. 1995), the zero-bias rest
  position, the undamped step threshold from that position, the damping
  `zeta_c` at which the band closes for each device, the pressures at which
  the gas damping reaches `zeta_c` and `2^(-1/4)`, and the band width in air
  with a constant and with a gap-dependent damping profile. Writes
  `results/damping_regimes.json`. Self-checks against the appendix:
  `zeta_c(0) = 0.395919`, the closed form of McLellan et al., and the band
  width `1.3482e-2` at `zeta = 0.1`.
- `make_sm_tables.py` now also emits Table S10 and the device table of the
  main text, `article/sections/tab_devices.tex`.
- `run_all.py` stage `damping`.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-08-26

Published 2026-08-28 as `10.5281/zenodo.22142711`. Note that
`10.5281/zenodo.21269555`, used as the concept DOI before this release, is in
fact the version DOI of 1.0.0; the concept DOI is `10.5281/zenodo.21269554`.
The manuscript now cites the 1.1.0 version DOI, since that is the archive its
numbers come from.

### Fixed

- **Thermal Lifshitz coefficient, corrected by a factor of 12.** The finite-
  temperature correction to the Casimir parameter used
  `c_L = 720 zeta(3)/pi^3 = 27.913`. The correct coefficient is the ratio of
  the classical (high-temperature) Casimir pressure
  `P_cl = zeta(3) k_B T/(4 pi a^3)` to the zero-temperature pressure
  `P_0 = pi^2 hbar c/(240 a^4)`, that is

      c_L = 60 zeta(3)/pi^3 = 2.3261 .

  The old value divided the classical term by the free-energy coefficient 720
  instead of the pressure coefficient 240 (factor 3) and dropped the 4 in
  `P_cl` (factor 4). The derivation is now Appendix A of the manuscript.
  Affects `src/physics.py`, `src/lifshitz_boundary.py`, `src/device_table.py`,
  `docs/lifshitz_notes.md`, and every downstream number:

  | Quantity | 1.0.0 | 1.1.0 |
  |---|---|---|
  | `kappa(100 nm)` | 1.219e-3 /K | 1.0158e-4 /K |
  | `dV_PI/V_PI` at `beta = 0.03`, 100/300 K | -2.88 % / -8.87 % | -0.237 % / -0.712 % |
  | `beta*` at 100/300 K | 0.074623 / 0.063412 | 0.081257 / 0.079914 |
  | Device `dV_PI` A/B/C/D at 300 K | -7.1/-13.6/-0.03/-5.7 % | -0.57/-1.07/-0.003/-0.46 % |

  The qualitative conclusions are unchanged: the shift is one-sided,
  destabilizing, vanishes at `beta = 0`, and grows toward the Casimir ceiling.
  The classical-limit bound is now itself sub-percent at sub-100-nm gaps,
  which makes it consistent with the low-temperature result
  `P = P_0 [1 + (16/3)(a/lambda_T)^4]` derived in the same appendix.

### Added

- `src/make_sm_tables.py` emits the Supplemental Material LaTeX tables
  directly from `results/` and `logs/`, so no number in the Supplemental
  Material is transcribed by hand.

### Changed

- `figures/fig3_lifshitz_shift.*` recomposed: with the corrected coefficient
  the three fold curves are indistinguishable in the `(alpha, beta)` plane, so
  the figure now reports the relative shift as a function of `beta`.
- Regenerated `results/lifshitz_boundaries.{npz,csv}`,
  `results/lifshitz_shift_summary.csv`, `results/device_table.{csv,json}`.
